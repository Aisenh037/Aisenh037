I'm Karan — B.Tech in Mathematics & Data Science Engineering from NIT Bhopal (2022–2026, CGPA 8.02 through the 6th semester). I like problems where the hard part isn't the model: messy data, regulation written in PDFs, users who speak to their phone instead of typing.

How I build

  • Backend-first. Spring Boot and FastAPI services where RBAC, idempotency, and audit trails are features, not afterthoughts.
  • Data before models. Forecasting starts at the ETL layer — ARIMA/XGBoost/LSTM come after the pipeline is trustworthy.
  • AI as a component. RAG, agents, and LLM features live behind the same engineering discipline as any other service.
